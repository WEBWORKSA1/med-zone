Questions about our calculators

How accurate are these calculators?

Each one uses a published, peer-reviewed formula and is tested against worked examples from its source. The math is exact; the formulas themselves are estimates for populations, so your real value can differ. That is why every page lists the limits of the method.

Do you store what I type?

No. Calculations run in your browser and nothing you enter is sent to us. If you copy a "link with my inputs", the values are stored only in that link.

Can I use them at work or in class?

Yes. Clinicians and students use them for quick double-checks and teaching. They do not replace clinical judgement, local protocols or a pharmacist's review.

Can I request a calculator?

Yes. Tell us which formula you need and where it is published. Sponsors can also fund a new calculator.
