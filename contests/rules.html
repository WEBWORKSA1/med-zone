NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING.

1. Organizer

The {{ site.contest.name }} (the "Contest") is organized by Med.Zone (the "Organizer"). Contact the Organizer through the contact form.

2. Contest period

Each Contest period runs for one calendar month. The current period is {{ site.contest.period }} and closes at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on {{ site.contest.closes }}.

3. Eligibility

Open to individuals who have reached the age of majority where they live and who reside in a country, state or province where this Contest is not prohibited. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Employees and contractors of the Organizer and their immediate families are not eligible.

4. How to enter

Complete a round of the Med.Zone health knowledge quiz and submit the entry form with your score, name, email address and location. Alternative entry: send your name, email, location and the answer to "What does the abbreviation PRN mean?" through the contact form with the topic "Contest". Alternative entries are treated as having a score equal to the highest score submitted that month. Limit one entry per person per Contest period; additional entries are void.

5. Winner selection

Within 14 days after the period closes, one potential winner will be selected by random draw from all eligible entries that share the highest submitted score. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries and the scores submitted. The potential winner will be contacted by email and must respond within 10 days or an alternate may be selected.

Residents of Canada must correctly answer, without assistance, a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question before being declared a winner.

6. Prize

One prize per period: {{ site.contest.prize }}. The prize is not transferable and no substitution is allowed except at the Organizer's discretion, in which case a prize of equal or greater value will be awarded. Taxes, if any, are the winner's responsibility.

7. Publicity

By accepting a prize, the winner agrees that their first name, last initial and region may be published on Med.Zone, unless prohibited by law.

8. Privacy

Entry information is used to administer the Contest and, if you opt in, to send the Med.Zone email. See our privacy policy.

9. General conditions

The Organizer may disqualify entries that are incomplete, fraudulent or generated by automated means, and may cancel, modify or suspend the Contest if fraud, technical failure or any other factor impairs its integrity. The Organizer is not responsible for lost, late or misdirected entries. By entering, participants release the Organizer from liability for any loss arising from participation or acceptance of a prize, to the extent permitted by law.

10. Governing law

These rules are governed by the laws of the Province of Québec and the federal laws of Canada applicable there, except where mandatory local law provides otherwise. For Québec residents: any litigation respecting the conduct or organization of a publicity contest may be submitted to the Régie des alcools, des courses et des jeux for a ruling. Any litigation respecting the awarding of a prize may be submitted to the Régie only for the purpose of helping the parties reach a settlement.

11. Winner list

Winners are listed on the contests page after each draw.
