Health information should work for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or have low vision.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we've built in

  • Text set in Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed by the Braille Institute for readers with low vision.
  • Full keyboard support, a skip link and visible focus outlines.
  • Labeled form fields, live regions that announce calculator results and clear error messages.
  • A dark theme and support for your device's reduced-motion setting.
  • Results never rely on color alone; every flag has a text label.
  • Print-friendly calculator results and reference sheets.

Known limitations

Some third-party content, such as embedded videos and ads, is outside our control and may not fully meet these standards.

Report a barrier

If something doesn't work for you, tell us what page you were on and what happened. We'll do our best to fix it or provide the information another way.
