Get more from a short appointment

Many primary-care visits last 15 to 20 minutes. Lead with your most important concern, bring every medicine bottle or a photo of the labels, and repeat the plan back in your own words before you leave. If you're seeing a new doctor, request your records in advance. Read the full guide: how to prepare for a doctor's appointment.
