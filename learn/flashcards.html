How to study with flashcards

Flashcards work because they make you retrieve information rather than reread it, which strengthens memory more than passive review. Space your sessions out: a few minutes a day beats an hour the night before an exam. Say the answer out loud before you flip, and keep the cards you miss in the next session.

Medical words are built from parts. Once you know that nephr/o is kidney and -ectomy means removal, "nephrectomy" needs no memorizing.
