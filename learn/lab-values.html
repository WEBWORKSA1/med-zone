Reading your results

Reference ranges are usually set so that 95 percent of healthy people fall inside them. That means about 1 in 20 healthy people will have a result just outside the range on any single test, so a slightly high or low value is often not a problem.

What matters most is the size of the change, the trend over time and how the result fits with your symptoms and other tests. Read our full guide on how to read blood test results.
