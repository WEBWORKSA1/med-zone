## How the fertile window works

An egg survives for about a day after ovulation, but sperm can survive for up to five days. That makes six days of each cycle fertile: the five days before ovulation and ovulation day itself. The highest chance of pregnancy comes from intercourse in the two days before ovulation and on the day itself.

## How the calculator estimates ovulation

The second half of the cycle (the luteal phase) is fairly steady at about 14 days. So ovulation is estimated at <span class="formula">cycle length - 14</span> days after the start of your period: day 14 in a 28-day cycle, day 18 in a 32-day cycle.

## Making the estimate more precise

- **Ovulation (LH) test strips** detect the hormone surge 24 to 36 hours before ovulation.
- **Cervical mucus** becomes clear and stretchy near ovulation.
- **Basal body temperature** rises after ovulation, confirming it happened.

## When to seek advice

See a clinician if you are under 35 and haven't conceived after 12 months of trying, or after 6 months if you are 35 or older, or sooner if your cycles are very irregular.
