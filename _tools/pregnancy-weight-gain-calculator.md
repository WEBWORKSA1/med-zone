## Recommended total gain (single pregnancy)

| Pre-pregnancy BMI | Total gain | Weekly in 2nd and 3rd trimesters |
|---|---|---|
| Under 18.5 | 12.5 to 18 kg (28 to 40 lb) | 0.44 to 0.58 kg (1 to 1.3 lb) |
| 18.5 to 24.9 | 11.5 to 16 kg (25 to 35 lb) | 0.35 to 0.50 kg (0.8 to 1 lb) |
| 25 to 29.9 | 7 to 11.5 kg (15 to 25 lb) | 0.23 to 0.33 kg (0.5 to 0.7 lb) |
| 30 or more | 5 to 9 kg (11 to 20 lb) | 0.17 to 0.27 kg (0.4 to 0.6 lb) |

For twins, the provisional ranges are 17 to 25 kg (37 to 54 lb) at a healthy BMI, 14 to 23 kg (31 to 50 lb) when overweight and 11 to 19 kg (25 to 42 lb) with obesity.

## Where the weight goes

Only part of the gain is the baby. The rest is the placenta, amniotic fluid, a larger uterus, extra blood and fluid, breast tissue and stored energy for breastfeeding.

## How the weekly estimate works

Gain in the first trimester is small, about 0.5 to 2 kg in total. From week 14 the calculator adds the weekly rate for your BMI group, so you can see whether your current gain is in the expected band.

## Talk to your care team if

You gain or lose weight suddenly, especially with swelling of the face or hands, headaches or vision changes, which can be signs of preeclampsia and need prompt assessment.
