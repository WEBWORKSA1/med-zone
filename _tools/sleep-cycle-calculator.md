## How sleep cycles work

Through the night your brain moves through repeating cycles of light sleep, deep (slow-wave) sleep and REM sleep. Each cycle averages about 90 minutes, though real cycles range from roughly 70 to 120 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle, when sleep is lighter, usually feels easier than being pulled out of deep sleep.

## How the calculator works

It counts back (or forward) in 90-minute blocks and adds about 15 minutes to fall asleep. Five cycles gives 7.5 hours of sleep and six gives 9 hours, which fits the recommendation that adults get **at least 7 hours a night**.

## Getting better sleep

- Keep a consistent wake time, including weekends.
- Get daylight soon after waking and dim screens in the hour before bed.
- Limit caffeine after early afternoon and alcohol close to bedtime.
- For shift work, keep the bedroom dark, cool and quiet, and protect the same sleep block across work days.

## When to see a clinician

Loud snoring with pauses in breathing, persistent daytime sleepiness, or insomnia lasting more than three months deserve medical attention. Sleep apnea and chronic insomnia are common and treatable.
