Med.Zone turns health numbers into understanding. We build free, fast, carefully sourced tools for anyone who has ever looked at a lab result, a blood pressure reading or a drug label and wondered what it means.

What we build

  • {{ site.tools.size }} medical calculators, each showing its formula, limits and sources.
  • Live public data: FDA drug labels and recalls, NLM condition and ICD-10 lookups, ClinicalTrials.gov studies and new PubMed research.
  • Guides and study tools: plain-language explainers, a quiz, flashcards and reference tables.

Who it's for

Patients and caregivers who want to understand their results, students preparing for exams, and clinicians who need a quick, transparent double-check.

How we work

Every calculator is built from a published, peer-reviewed formula and tested against worked examples. Every guide links its sources. We say plainly what a tool can't tell you. Read the full editorial policy.

How we're funded

Med.Zone is free to use. It is funded by display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labeled sponsorships, referral fees from partners when readers ask to be connected with them, and reader donations. Sponsors and partners never influence formulas, results or editorial content.

Independence

Med.Zone is an independent website. It is not a healthcare provider, insurer or government agency, and it is not affiliated with any organization whose data or videos it displays. See our trademark and copyright disclosure.
