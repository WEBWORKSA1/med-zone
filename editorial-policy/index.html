Health information has to be right. This page explains how we try to make sure ours is, and what we do when we get something wrong.

Sources we rely on

  1. Clinical practice guidelines from national and international bodies (for example ACC/AHA, ADA, KDIGO, ESC, USPSTF, WHO, NICE).
  2. Systematic reviews and peer-reviewed original research, usually found through PubMed.
  3. Government health agencies (NIH, CDC, FDA, NLM and equivalents) and official product labeling.

We avoid relying on press releases, blogs or commercial sources for medical claims. Every calculator and guide lists its references.

How calculators are built

  • We implement the formula exactly as published, including units and any sex- or age-specific terms.
  • We test against worked examples from the source or from independent references before publishing.
  • Each page explains the formula, how to interpret the result and the method's known limitations.
  • Calculations run in your browser; we do not collect what you enter.

Review and updates

Content is written and checked against its sources by the Med.Zone editorial team, and the date of the last check appears on each page. We revisit pages when major guidelines change. We are building a panel of licensed medical reviewers; qualified clinicians can apply here. Pages reviewed by a named clinician will say so.

Use of AI tools

We may use AI tools to help draft, summarize or code. Nothing is published until an editor has checked every medical claim against the cited sources and tested every calculator.

Corrections

If you find an error, please tell us. We review every report, correct confirmed errors promptly and note significant corrections on the page.

Advertising and independence

Advertising, sponsorships and partner relationships are kept separate from editorial decisions. Sponsors cannot change formulas, results or content, and sponsored placements are always labeled. Display ads are selected by Google, not by us.

Not medical advice

Med.Zone provides general information and educational tools. Read our full medical disclaimer.
