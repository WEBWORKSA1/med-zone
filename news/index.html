About this feed

Med.Zone collects headlines from public RSS feeds published by research institutions and science newsrooms, including the U.S. National Institutes of Health, the FDA and ScienceDaily. We show the headline, date and a short summary and send you to the original publisher for the full story.

News about a single study is rarely the final word. Before changing anything about your health, check the study type and size, and talk to your clinician. See the research finder to read the studies themselves.
