Information you send us

When you submit a form (contact, newsletter, get matched, careers, contest, pledge or advertising), we receive what you enter plus the page you sent it from and basic referral information. Forms are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, an email form service. We use this information to reply, deliver what you asked for and run our programs.

Get matched requests

If you ask to be connected with a provider, advisor or employer and give consent, we share your request with up to three relevant partners, who may contact you. Partners may pay us a referral fee. We do not sell your information to list brokers. Please don't include sensitive medical details in forms.

Calculators and lookups

Calculator inputs stay on your device. Searches in the drug, condition, trial and research tools are sent directly from your browser to the public services that answer them (openFDA, the U.S. National Library of Medicine, ClinicalTrials.gov), under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and local storage

We store a few preferences in your browser's local storage, such as dark mode, your cookie choice and your best quiz score. These never leave your device.

Advertising

Med.Zone uses Google AdSense to show ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's certified consent message where required. Choosing "Essential only" in our notice asks Google to show non-personalized ads.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

How long we keep information

We keep form messages as long as needed to respond and run the related program, and generally no longer than 24 months, unless the law requires otherwise.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California law, Canada's PIPEDA or Québec's Law 25), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your information, to withdraw consent and to object to certain processing. Send a privacy request and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Med.Zone is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Contest entry requires the age of majority.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
