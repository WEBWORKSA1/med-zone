How clinical trials work

PhaseMain questionTypical size
Phase 1Is it safe, and what dose?20 to 100 people
Phase 2Does it work, and what are the side effects?Up to several hundred
Phase 3Is it better than current treatment?Hundreds to thousands
Phase 4What happens after approval, long term?Thousands

Before you join, ask

  • What is being tested, and what are the alternatives?
  • Could I receive a placebo? Can I keep my current treatment?
  • How many visits, tests and how much time are involved?
  • Are travel, parking or time costs covered? Who pays if I'm harmed?
  • Can I leave at any time? (You always can.)
