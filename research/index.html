Weighing the evidence

Not every study carries the same weight. From strongest to weakest, roughly:

  1. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses pool many studies on one question.
  2. Randomized controlled trials assign treatments by chance, which limits bias.
  3. Cohort and case-control studies follow or compare groups and can show links, not proof of cause.
  4. Case reports, lab and animal studies generate ideas that still need testing in people.

When you read a headline, check the study type, how many people took part, and whether the result was an absolute or a relative change. Our guide on spotting reliable health information walks through it.
